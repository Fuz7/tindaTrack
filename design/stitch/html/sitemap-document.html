# TindaTrack Sitemap: Sari-Sari Store Tracker ## 1. Home Page (Smart Calculator) *Primary function: Rapid sales entry and point-of-sale.* * **Quick Entry Calculator**: Numeric keypad for price input. * **Active Cart**: List of items being tallied. * **Transaction Controls**: * Clear Cart * Complete Sale (Link to Transaction Confirmation) * **Inventory Integration**: Search bar to pull specific products into the calculator. ## 2. Products Page (Inventory Management) *Primary function: Managing store stock.* * **Product List**: * Search & Filter (by category, e.g., Snacks, Drinks, Canned Goods). * Stock Status (In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock). * **Product Actions**: * Add New Product (Name, Buy Price, Sell Price, Category, Initial Stock). * Edit Product (Update prices or stock levels). * **Quick Restock**: One-tap increment buttons for common items. ## 3. Transactions Page (History) *Primary function: Reviewing past sales.* * **Daily Log**: List of transactions sorted by time (Newest first). * **Transaction Details**: Breakdown of items sold and total amount. * **Quick Actions**: * Void/Refund Transaction. * Print/Share Receipt (Optional). * **Search**: Find specific transactions by date or item. ## 4. Analytics Page (Business Insights) *Primary function: Monitoring profit and trends.* * **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**: * Total Sales (Daily/Weekly/Monthly). * Estimated Profit. * **Trends & Charts**: * Best-Selling Items (Top 5). * Sales Volume over time. * **Stock Alerts**: Summary of items that need restocking. ## 5. Global Navigation (Sitemap Structure) * **Bottom Navigation Bar**: [Home] | [Products] | [Transactions] | [Analytics] * **Top Bar**: Store Name, Settings, and Profile.
